# NyaySetu — Know Your Rights and Laws

A web-based legal aid platform connecting citizens with legal information and verified lawyers across 9 practice areas — built to bridge the justice gap for people who can't easily access or afford legal help.

🔗 **Live Site:** https://tanusdalvi.github.io/nyaysetu/
---

## 📌 Problem Statement
Millions of people in India lack access to affordable legal advice. Legal awareness is especially low in rural and semi-urban areas, traditional consultation is slow and costly, and existing online resources are scattered and confusing. NyaySetu addresses this by making legal help simple, centralized, and accessible.
---

## ✨ Features
- Covers **9 legal domains**: Civil, Family, Criminal, Corporate, Employment, Personal Injury, Real Estate, Immigration, Intellectual Property
- **Multilingual support** — English, Hindi, and Marathi versions of every page
- Clean, **responsive design** — works across mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Detailed, easy-to-understand service descriptions for each legal domain
---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- HTML5
- CSS3 (responsive, mobile-first)
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Deployed via GitHub Pages
